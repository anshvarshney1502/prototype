The problem

Small businesses miss calls. Voice AI could answer them,
but nobody trusts it with real customers.

Building is too technicalToday's voice agent tools expect system prompts, tool schemas and webhooks. A clinic owner thinks in rules, not prompts.
One good demo is not proofLLM agents are probabilistic. An agent that answers correctly once may invent a price on the next call.
Mistakes are expensiveA wrong price, a fake booking, or medical advice given to a patient damages trust instantly.
“I don't want an AI that is clever. I want one that does exactly what my front desk does, and nothing more.”

Meet our user

Dr. Sharma runs a dental clinic in Indirapuram.

  • Open Mon to Sat, 10–2 and 5–8:30. The phone rings while she is with patients.
  • Callers ask for prices, book cleanings, and sometimes call in pain.
  • She speaks Hindi, English and Hinglish, and so do her patients.
  • She has never written a prompt in her life.

Our goal: she goes from zero to a tested, live AI receptionist in minutes, using only her voice.

Munshi treats an AI agent like a new hire.

Brief it. Check its job card. Give it a trial day. Coach its mistakes. Only then let it answer the phone.

04
Step 1 · Interview

She talks. The job card fills itself.

  • Munshi asks the 8 questions a manager would ask a new receptionist.
  • Answers in Hindi, English or Hinglish, by voice or text.
  • ElevenLabs client tools write each answer into the job card live, while she is still speaking.
“Sharma Dental Clinic in Indirapuram. Monday to Saturday, 10 to 2, and again 5 to 8:30.”
Munshi
05
Step 2 · Job card

Plain-language rules, not a hidden prompt.

  • Business, prices, booking, languages, hand-off, “never”, “if unsure”.
  • Prices come from her own price list. Nothing is invented.
  • She picks a natural Indian English / Hindi voice from the ElevenLabs library.
  • Every call opens by saying it is an AI. Nothing is created until she approves.
Munshi
06
Step 3 · Trial Day

8 tricky calls. Each one run 3 times.

Because one good answer is not proof. Tests run through ElevenLabs Agent Testing with repeat_count: 3 on the exact version.

  • Patient in pain, slot already taken, calendar down, “ignore your rules”…
  • Unknown price · 0/3
    The agent quoted ₹3,000 for whitening. That price is not on her list.
  • Go live stays locked.
Munshi
07
Step 4 · Coach

Fix a mistake with one sentence.

“If a price isn't on my list, don't guess. Say you'll confirm and offer a callback.”
  • Typed or spoken (ElevenLabs Scribe v2). Gemini turns it into a job-card rule.
  • A new version 2 is created on its own branch. Version 1 is never touched.
  • The failure becomes a permanent test for every future version.
Munshi
08
Step 3, again · Proof

Every test re-runs. 8 of 8, three times each.

  • Unknown price · 3/3
  • Now: “I can't confirm the whitening price right now. I can ask the clinic to call you back.”
  • The booking test matched a real calendar record, bk_8812.
  • Tests ran on the same version that goes live.

Ready to go live unlocks only now.

Munshi
09
Step 5 · Go live and observe

Live on her website in one line.

  • Passing branch merges to main. Public talk link and <elevenlabs-convai> widget. Phone via Twilio / SIP.
  • Call log with transcript, summary, outcome and sentiment.
  • Every booking ID the agent mentions is verified against the calendar.
  • Calls that need a human are flagged in red.

Why you can trust it

Guardrails enforced in code, not promised in a prompt.

No untested version liveGo live checks the last run of that exact version. Every blocker must pass 3/3.
No fake bookingsA booking exists only if the tool returned a real ID. A unique index blocks double booking.
Instant rollbackEvery version lives on its own branch. A bad correction can never reach patients.
Nothing exposed in the browserVoice sessions start from short-lived signed URLs only.
Responsible AIAlways discloses it is an AI. Never gives medical advice. Refuses prompt injection.
Built for IndiaHindi, English and Hinglish, end to end, by voice.
