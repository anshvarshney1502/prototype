Munshi: 3.5-minute pitch script

About 470 words at a calm 135 words per minute. Slide numbers match the deck.
Slide 1 · Title 0:00–0:12

Hi, I'm Ansh from team DOT DOT COM. This is Munshi. It lets anyone hire a voice receptionist by explaining the job out loud, not by writing prompts.

Slide 2 · Problem 0:12–0:35

Small businesses miss calls all day. Voice AI could answer them, but there are three problems. Building an agent is too technical. One good demo is not proof, because these agents are probabilistic. And one wrong price or fake booking destroys trust.

Slide 3 · User 0:35–0:50

Meet Dr. Sharma. She runs a dental clinic in Indirapuram, speaks Hindi and English, and has never written a prompt. Her phone rings while she is treating patients.

Slide 4 · Solution 0:50–1:05

Munshi treats an AI agent like a new hire. Brief it, check its job card, give it a trial day, coach its mistakes, and only then let it answer the phone. That maps exactly to the brief: write, review, test, refine, deploy, observe.

Slide 5 · Interview 1:05–1:22

She presses one button and talks. Munshi asks the eight questions a manager would ask. Using ElevenLabs client tools, every answer lands in the job card live, while she is still speaking.

Slide 6 · Job card 1:22–1:37

She sees plain rules, not a hidden prompt: prices from her own list, when to hand off, what to never do. She picks a voice. Nothing is created until she approves.

Slide 7 · Trial Day 1:37–2:00

Now the trial day. Eight tricky calls, each run three times with ElevenLabs Agent Testing. Seven pass. But on "Unknown price", the agent told a caller whitening costs three thousand rupees. That price isn't on her list. So Go live stays locked.

Slide 8 · Coach 2:00–2:20

She fixes it with one sentence: "If a price isn't on my list, don't guess. Offer a callback." Munshi turns it into a rule, creates version two on its own branch, and saves this failure as a permanent test.

Slide 9 · Proof 2:20–2:35

Every test re-runs. Eight out of eight, three times each, on the exact version going live. Now the agent says it can't confirm the price and offers a callback. Only now does Go live unlock.

Slide 10 · Live 2:35–2:52

One line of code puts it on her website. Every call is logged with a summary and outcome, every booking ID is checked against the real calendar, and calls that need her are flagged.

Slides 11–12 · Tech and trust 2:52–3:15

This is a real product: ElevenLabs Agents, Agent Testing, versioning, Scribe and Flash TTS, with Next.js and MongoDB. And the guardrails are in code: no untested version goes live, no fake bookings, instant rollback, and it always says it's an AI.

Slides 13–14 · Vision and close 3:15–3:30

The same loop works for any front desk: appointments, leads, support, collections. Our belief is simple: don't trust an AI agent because it sounds good. Trust it because it passed its trial day. Thank you.

Delivery tips. On slides 7 to 9, slow down: the red ₹3,000 failure turning green is the moment judges remember. If you have a live demo slot, show slides 7 → 8 → 9 live in the app instead of the screenshots. Keep the final line as your last words, then pause.
